03 / EVERY CHANNELTHE LONG VIEW

Every door sellers use.
One place for you.

Email is the start. The vision reaches the inboxes, phones, events, and loose ends where real vendor conversations happen.

Connect Gmail. Manciple detects sales outreach, scores it against your buying needs, extracts claims, and drafts replies for your review. Read and draft permissions only; it does not send for you.

GMAIL → RELEVANCE → CLAIMS → DRAFTS

Bring messages and InMail into the same buyer context. The plan starts with notification emails and user-provided imports, then explores opt-in, read-only computer use. You send any reply yourself.

MESSAGES → FIT → SUGGESTED REPLY

Manciple Answers would take unknown calls, learn who's calling and why, decline unwanted pitches, or question vendors that match your brief. You get a useful summary instead of another voicemail.

CALL → CONVERSATION → SUMMARY

Unknown senders and promotional texts would be scored against your intent, with scams flagged and unwanted outreach easy to decline.

TEXT → SCREEN → KEEP OR DECLINE

Your Manciple Code would replace the badge scan with a structured front door. A vendor reads the brief you choose to share, answers your questions, and enters the same comparison as everyone else.

EVENT → CODE → STRUCTURED OFFER

The same email pipeline, extended beyond Gmail so your buying brief follows you into the other major business inbox.

OUTLOOK → SAME BUYER PROFILE

Forward a pitch, share a screenshot, snap a brochure, or capture vendor outreach from shared Slack and Teams channels or calendar invites. It should still land in the same decision room.
